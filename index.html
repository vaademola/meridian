The Agency

A small firm, by design.

Meridian was founded on a simple conviction: the sale of a significant home deserves the attention of a principal, not a call centre. We take on a limited number of mandates each year so that every client works directly with the people who know their property best.

Our approach is discreet, unhurried and entirely personal — from the first valuation to the final signature, and long after.
